Your model. Your call.

AI is optional: choose No AI and Coral stays a fast launcher with notes, reminders, clipboard, emoji and screenshots, with every AI control hidden. Chat, screen questions and translation all go through the provider you choose. Keep several provider profiles and switch from the footer. Press Tab to send anything you typed to chat, and Esc to step back to the launcher without losing the conversation.

Local

Download a recommended model in one click, or bring your own GGUF. Coral starts and manages llama-server, adds a vision projector if you have one, and frees the memory after 10 idle minutes by default.

Custom

Point Coral at any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, hosted or on your own network. The key is optional for local servers. With a cloud service, your messages and screen content go to that provider.
